Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Success mobile application ("Success", "the App", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use it. We have built Success with privacy as a first principle.

1. The short version

We do not collect personal data. We do not show advertisements. We do not use third-party analytics or tracking services. We do not sell, rent, or share user information — because we do not have any to share.

2. Information we do not collect

  • We do not require you to create an account.
  • We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or location.
  • We do not include third-party advertising SDKs or trackers.
  • We do not profile you or build a behavioral record of your activity.

3. Information stored on your device

Any preferences you set (such as your favorites, reading history, or notification preferences) are stored locally on your device. This information stays on your device. It is not transmitted to us or to any third party. If you delete the app, this local data is removed with it.

4. Apple services and in-app purchases

Success is distributed through the Apple App Store. Apple may collect information related to app downloads and crashes in accordance with its own policies. We do not control and are not responsible for Apple's data practices. Please review Apple's privacy policy for details.

Success offers optional paid subscriptions ($2.99/month and $29.99/year, each with a 3-day free trial for eligible new subscribers) and a Lifetime purchase ($49.99). All purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details (card number, billing address, or Apple ID). The App uses Apple's StoreKit framework on your device to verify your purchase status — these checks happen entirely on your device. We do not send purchase receipts to any server we control or to any third party for validation. Apple may share with us aggregated, anonymized sales information as part of the standard App Store analytics provided to developers.

5. Sharing content

When you share a quote or image from Success, the image may include a link to https://scss.app/get so that recipients can find the app. Sharing happens only when you initiate it through the iOS share sheet, and the destination of any shared content is controlled by you.

6. Children's privacy

Success is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App or for legal reasons. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will be highlighted within the App or on this website.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach us at hello@scss.app.
